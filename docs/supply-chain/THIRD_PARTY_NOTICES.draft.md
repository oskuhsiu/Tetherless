# Third-party notices draft

Candidate baseline: 3dd8641e84698b97f96d53a6c53ed8c6ca4675bd

Preserve original authors, copyright notices, upstream names in attribution, full license texts and modification notices. No rights grant is created by this draft.

This notice bundle is incomplete until final binary/source correspondence, per-crate notices, Unicorn compatibility and ADI rights have been resolved.
